The shop

Just outside historic downtown Harrisburg

Keystone Tattoo Studio sits at Main Street, just outside historic downtown Harrisburg, PA. The artists are available to discuss any ideas you have — that’s the shop’s own standing invitation, and the consultation costs nothing.

The room works two ways: appointments for planned pieces, and walk-ins when the chairs are open. Either way, the work in the Flash Book above is the work that walks out of here.
